# Domain Summary: People (33%)

<!-- group: Domain Summaries | order: 1 | summary: Vision, conflict, leadership, motivation, emotional intelligence, stakeholders, knowledge transfer, and communication. -->

## Task 1 — Develop a common vision
- Co-create the vision with team and stakeholders (workshops, product box, elevator pitch); anchor it to the business case.
- Keep it **visible** (vision board, roadmap, information radiators) and **current** (revisit when markets change).
- Identify **root causes of misalignment** — e.g., sponsors with conflicting success criteria.
- Translate vision into measurable outcomes (OKRs, KPIs, benefits).
- Explain the **why**, not just the what: people commit to purpose, not to instructions alone.

## Task 2 — Manage conflicts
| Mode | Use when | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Collaborate / problem solve | Time and relationships allow; important issue | Win-win, lasting |
| Compromise / reconcile | Need a quick, acceptable solution; equal power | Lose-lose, partial |
| Smooth / accommodate | Preserve harmony; issue minor or temporary | Temporary |
| Force / direct | Emergencies, safety, legal/compliance | Win-lose |
| Withdraw / avoid | Cool-off needed; trivial issue | Postponed |

- Common sources: schedules, priorities, and resources (personality is least common).
- Address conflict **early and privately**; focus on the problem, not the person.
- Ground rules in the **team charter**; clear roles (one "A" in RACI).
- Interpersonal complaints: hear both sides, then bring the people together to agree behaviors. Cross-team conflict: build empathy (shadowing, shared goals).

## Task 3 — Lead the project team
- **Servant leadership:** remove impediments, coach, facilitate, protect focus.
- **Tuckman:** forming → storming → norming → performing → adjourning.
- **Situational leadership:** directing → coaching → supporting → delegating as competence and commitment grow.
- **Motivation:** Maslow; Herzberg (hygiene vs. motivators); McGregor X/Y; McClelland; Vroom expectancy; Pink (autonomy, mastery, purpose).
- **Emotional intelligence:** self-awareness, self-regulation, motivation, empathy, social skills.
- **Feedback:** timely, specific, private for criticism, a dialogue — avoid watered-down feedback.
- **One-on-ones:** the employee's meeting; listen far more than you talk.
- **Recognition:** team-based, tied to outcomes; avoid rewards that pit teammates against each other.
- **Sustainable pace**; stable, dedicated teams; T-shaped skills.
- **Psychological safety:** people can raise problems and mistakes without fear. The PM models it (admits own mistakes, thanks bad-news bearers).

## Tasks 4–6 — Stakeholders
- Identify early and continuously; stakeholder register is living.
- Analysis tools: **power/interest grid** (manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed, monitor), **salience model** (power, legitimacy, urgency), **engagement assessment matrix** (unaware, resistant, neutral, supportive, leading — current vs. desired).
- Engage resistant, influential stakeholders **more**, not less; listen first.
- Align expectations: make them explicit, find conflicts, facilitate prioritization (MoSCoW, Kano), show trade-offs honestly.
- Manage expectations: monitor satisfaction, communicate early (especially bad news), route change requests properly, forecast with ranges.
- Watch for hidden stakeholders: end users, operations and support, regulators, communities affected by the work.

## Task 7 — Knowledge transfer
- **Explicit** knowledge → documents, wikis, lessons learned register/repository.
- **Tacit** knowledge → mentoring, shadowing, pairing, storytelling, communities of practice.
- Lessons learned: capture **throughout**; transfer to the organizational repository at closure.
- Handover to operations: plan early, co-create runbooks, train, support period.
- Vendors: make knowledge transfer a contract deliverable.

## Task 8 — Communication
- Communications management plan: who needs what, when, how, from whom.
- Methods: **interactive** (urgent, complex, decisions), **push** (targeted distribution), **pull** (large audiences, reference).
- Channels = n(n − 1)/2.
- Transparency beats spin: report bad news early, and verify status with objective data (avoid "watermelon" reporting).
- Feedback loops confirm understanding; tailor format to the audience (executive summaries, milestone charts).
