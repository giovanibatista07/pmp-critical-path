# The 2026 PMP Exam at a Glance

## Format

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Total questions | 180 (170 scored + 10 unscored pretest questions) |
| Time | 240 minutes |
| Breaks | Two 10-minute breaks |
| Delivery | Test center or online proctored |
| Blueprint | The **2026 PMP Examination Content Outline (ECO)** — not a single textbook |

The 2026 exam launched in **July 2026**. Industry prep providers report that roughly **60% of questions reflect agile or hybrid approaches** and about 40% predictive. Many questions are approach-neutral: they test judgment (people, value, ethics) that applies everywhere.

## Domains and weights

| Domain | 2021 ECO | **2026 ECO** | Tasks |
|---|---|---|---|
| People | 42% | **33%** | 8 |
| Process | 50% | **41%** | 10 |
| Business Environment | 8% | **26%** | 8 |

**The big shift:** Business Environment more than tripled. Expect many questions on governance, compliance (security, safety, sustainability, regulation), change control, risk, organizational change, and external change (including AI).

## All 26 tasks

### Domain I — People (33%)
1. Develop a common vision
2. Manage conflicts
3. Lead the project team
4. Engage stakeholders
5. Align stakeholder expectations
6. Manage stakeholder expectations
7. Help ensure knowledge transfer
8. Plan and manage communication

### Domain II — Process (41%)
1. Develop an integrated project management plan and plan delivery
2. Develop and manage project scope
3. Help ensure value-based delivery
4. Plan and manage resources
5. Plan and manage procurement
6. Plan and manage finance
7. Plan and optimize quality of products/deliverables
8. Plan and manage schedule
9. Evaluate project status
10. Manage project closure

### Domain III — Business Environment (26%)
1. Define and establish project governance
2. Plan and manage project compliance
3. Manage and control changes
4. Remove impediments and manage issues
5. Plan and manage risk
6. Continuous improvement
7. Support organizational change
8. Evaluate external business environment changes

## Question types you may see

- Multiple choice (single answer) — still the majority
- Multiple response ("Select two" / "Select three")
- Matching and enhanced matching
- Drag-and-drop, point-and-click (hotspot), pull-down lists
- **New in 2026:** case/scenario questions (several questions about one longer scenario) and graphic-based questions (charts, boards, diagrams)

## PMBOK Guide 8th edition (Nov 2025) — the vocabulary behind the exam

**6 principles:** Adopt a holistic view · Focus on value · Embed quality · Lead accountably · Integrate sustainability · Build empowered teams

**7 performance domains:** Governance · Scope · Schedule · Finance · Stakeholders · Resources · Risk

**5 focus areas:** Initiating · Planning · Executing · Monitoring and Controlling · Closing (about 40 non-prescriptive processes)

New emphasis: AI in project work (predictive risk analysis, automated scheduling, resource modeling), sustainability, finance and value realization, and a PMO focused on value rather than compliance.

## How this app maps to the exam

Every question is tagged with its **domain and ECO task**, its **approach** (Agile, Predictive, Hybrid, Any) and its **difficulty**. The bank's 600 questions (**198 People / 246 Process / 156 Business Environment**) match the 33/41/26 weighting exactly, with 19–27 questions per task. It also includes the new formats: **9 case-study sets (32 questions)**, **28 chart and table questions** (plus exhibits in 6 of the case studies), **31 multiple-response items** and **59 calculations**. Of the questions tied to a specific approach, two-thirds are agile or hybrid, matching the exam's tilt. Full-length simulations run 180 questions in 240 minutes, split into three 60-question sections with two optional 10-minute breaks, like the real test.
