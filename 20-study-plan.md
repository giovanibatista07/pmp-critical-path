# 8-Week Study Plan

<!-- group: Study Plan | order: 1 | summary: A week-by-week plan to go from first read to exam-ready, using this app's practice, review, and exam modes. -->

## Before you start

- Confirm eligibility and the 35 contact hours of project management education required by PMI; book your exam for about 8–10 weeks out.
- Take a **30-question diagnostic** in Exam mode (no hints) to find your weak domains.

## Weekly rhythm (about 8–10 hours per week)

| Day | Activity |
|---|---|
| 2 days | Read the relevant study guide pages and your reference material |
| 3 days | 25–40 practice questions for that week's tasks — answer, then reveal and read every option note |
| 1 day | Review: clear everything **due for spaced review**, then flagged questions |
| 1 day | Rest or light formula review |

## Readiness targets

- **75%+** on two consecutive 60-question simulations, and
- no domain below **70%** in Progress, and
- you can explain **why** each wrong option is wrong for questions you miss.

## Tips for using this app

- **Commit before revealing.** Selecting an answer never shows whether it's right — press **Reveal answer** when you're ready.
- **Use hints only in Practice mode.** Exam mode hides them, like the real test.
- **Flag** anything you guessed on, even if you got it right.
- **Shuffle options** is on by default so you learn the idea, not the letter.
- **Spaced review** brings missed questions back after 1, 3, 7 and 16 days. Clear the "due today" list before starting new questions.
- **Sit at least one full 180-question simulation.** It uses three sections with optional breaks, and a finished section is locked, so you practise pacing: about 80 minutes per section.
- **Back up progress** with Copy progress (Progress page). It's stored in this browser only.
- **Using another practice book?** Check the *Practice-Book Errata* page before trusting a surprising answer.
